Crate Sort 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

🔒 Data Not Collected

Crate Sort 3D does not collect, transmit to us, store on any server of ours, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up and no backend of ours. Your level progress, coins, lives and settings are stored only on your device.

What we collect

Nothing of our own. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs we operate, and uses no advertising identifier. We never see your data because it never reaches us.

Your progress & where it lives

Your level, coins, lives and daily-streak state are stored locally on your device using the platform's own private app storage. If your device's own backup (iCloud, Android backup) is enabled, that backup is governed by Apple or Google, not by us — we have no access to it.

Google Play services (Android)

The Android app includes the Google Play In-App Review API (for rating prompts), Google Play Billing (wired for future purchases) and Android's WorkManager/notification system (used only to tell you locally when your lives have refilled). These Google/Android system libraries may exchange their own diagnostic data with Google to keep their services working, under Google's own privacy policy. We do not add any analytics or advertising SDK of our own.

Notifications

Crate Sort 3D sends exactly one kind of local notification — that your lives are full again — and only if you allow notifications and the app is in the background with lives still regenerating. It is scheduled and shown entirely on your device.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA & others)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, delete or sell, and we do not sell or "share" personal information. You retain full control of your data directly on your device: deleting the app removes all local data, including your save file.

Children

Crate Sort 3D is not directed at children, shows no ads, and knowingly collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version here.
