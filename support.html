Frequently asked

How do I play?
Tap any crate on top of a stack to move it into your 7-slot tray. Collect three matching crates in the tray and they clear. Empty every stack to clear the dock and move to the next level.

What happens if my tray fills up?
If the tray reaches 7 crates without completing a triple, the level fails and you lose one life. Plan which stacks you tap, and save coins for a shuffle if you get stuck.

What are lives, and how do they refill?
You have up to 5 lives. Losing a level costs one life; lives refill automatically over time. You'll get a notification when they're full again, if you allow notifications.

What does the shuffle button do?
It spends coins to reshuffle the crates still on the dock, without changing how many of each type remain — handy when you're stuck.

Where is my progress stored?
Only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours. See our Privacy Policy.

Does Crate Sort 3D need an internet connection?
No, the core game is fully playable offline.
